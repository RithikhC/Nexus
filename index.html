What you just saw

The grid runs on everyone being different

A transformer is never built to supply every home at once, because people don't all do the same thing at the same time. That spread is called diversity, and the whole network is sized around it.

Car charging breaks that

A home charger pulls about as much power as the rest of the house combined, and everyone plugs in when they get home from work. Suddenly all the homes are doing the same thing at the same time.

Nobody actually needs it all at once

A car home at 6pm and leaving at 8am has fourteen hours to absorb four hours of charging. Smart charging simply spreads that out, giving power first to whoever leaves soonest.

About the numbers. This is a teaching model of one street, not a study of a real network. The shapes and the comparison are the point; the exact percentages follow from the assumptions listed in the paper, and a real feeder would differ. Nothing here is a measurement.
